💡 Local Tips

🚆 Transportation

Buy a Trenitalia rail pass for unlimited train travel across Italy. Much cheaper than individual tickets. Venice uses vaporetto (water buses) - get a 3-day pass.

🗣️ Language

English is widely spoken in tourist areas, especially Rome, Venice, and Florence. Learn basic phrases: Grazie (thanks), Prego (welcome), Scusi (excuse me), Dov'è (Where is).

🌞 Best Time

May is perfect - warm weather (70-80°F), manageable crowds before summer tourists, spring flowers blooming. Avoid July-August (hot, crowded, expensive).

👥 Crowds

Colosseum and Vatican busiest 11 AM - 4 PM. Book tickets online in advance. Visit early morning (9 AM) or late afternoon (4 PM) for shorter lines.

💵 Currency

Euro (€). ATMs widely available - withdraw cash rather than exchange. Credit cards accepted at most restaurants/shops. Smaller places (trattorias) may prefer cash.

🛡️ Safety

Rome, Venice, Florence are safe for tourists. Petty theft common in crowded areas (trains, attractions) - watch backpacks. Use hotel safes for valuables.
