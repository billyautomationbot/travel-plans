💡 Local Tips

🚆 Transportation

Get a T-money card (rechargeable transit card) at convenience stores. Use subway for everything - cheap and efficient. Airport bus to Myeongdong: ~₩10,000.

🗣️ Language

English widely spoken in tourist areas. Learn basic: Annyeonghaseyo (hello), Gamsahamnida (thank you), Eodi isseoyo (where is it), Sillyehamnida (excuse me).

🌞 Best Time

October is PERFECT - fall foliage, mild weather (60-70°F), fewer crowds than summer, great food season with ginseng chicken soup.

👥 Crowds

Popular spots busy 11 AM - 4 PM. Go early morning (9 AM) or evening. Avoid lunch hours at famous restaurants.

💵 Currency

Korean Won (₩). ATMs everywhere. Most places accept cards, but small old places want cash. 1 USD ≈ 1,200 KRW.

🛡️ Safety

Seoul is very safe. Petty theft rare but watch bags in crowded markets. Public transit is the safest, fastest way to explore.
